# Bison + Crow Milk content drop

This focused content pass adds two requested pieces of wildlife flavor without changing save schema version or human combat.

- A five-animal bison herd now grazes in the high meadow. The visual base is Quaternius' animated Bull (CC0), fetched reproducibly by the existing animal asset pipeline and reskinned/scaled as Alderwatch bison.
- Bison use the existing persistent wildlife health/death/carcass system and yield venison plus hide.
- Crows now yield one **Crow crop** from their carcass.
- **Crow milk** is crafted at a campfire from one Crow crop plus one Wild herb.
- Crow milk is edible as a six-minute tonic: +15 maximum health, +25 stamina, and an immediate 15-health recovery.

Yes, it is called Crow milk.
