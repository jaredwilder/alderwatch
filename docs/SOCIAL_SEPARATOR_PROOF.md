# Alderwatch Social Separator Proof

This note records the exact mathematical contract implemented by the realm-society layer. It is deliberately narrower than claiming one million unrestricted AI minds. The goal is to identify which social structure can be represented exactly at massive scale, which information is preserved, and where individual promotion is required.

## 1. Household correlation before exchangeability

The million-person layer has eight public citizen phases. A four-person household therefore has an ordered microstate in `S^4`, with `|S|=8`, so there are `8^4 = 4096` ordered family states.

For the declared household interface, member labels inside the household are irrelevant but household composition is not. Quotienting by permutations of the four members gives the stars-and-bars orbit count

`C(4 + 8 - 1, 8 - 1) = C(11, 7) = 330`.

The household certificate is the eight-entry phase-count vector summing to four. The Court enumerates all 4096 ordered microstates under every public population event and checks that equal household certificates always have the same successor certificate and additive public output. Household correlation is therefore retained exactly rather than flattened into a realm-wide histogram.

## 2. Sparse structural relationships

There are `1,048,576 / 4 = 262,144` households. Each 4,096-person population shard contains exactly 1,024 households, indexed by a ten-bit local household ordinal.

Three relationship families are generated by XOR perfect matchings on those ten bits:

- trade: `h -> h xor 1`
- rivalry: `h -> h xor 2`
- oath: `h -> h xor 512`

Each map is fixed-point-free and involutive, so every household has exactly one neighbor of each kind and every edge is recovered symmetrically from either endpoint. The complete million-person realm therefore has exactly

`262,144 * 3 / 2 = 393,216`

undirected structural household edges, while storing no adjacency list. Relationship lookup is O(1), graph degree is exactly three, and graph size is O(households), not O(households^2).

This is not a claim that all human relationships have degree three. These are the persistent structural ties that remain in the compressed substrate. Player-created friendships, grudges, marriages, debts, inventories, quest relations, or other unique facts must be added as sparse explicit exceptions when they become semantically load-bearing.

## 3. Ward boundary state

A population shard is treated as one dormant social ward. Everything outside a ward may communicate with its interior only through four finite public channels:

`B = {kin, market, watch, guild}`

with each channel taking one of four levels `{0,1,2,3}`. Thus the complete boundary state space has fixed size

`|B| = 4^4 = 256`.

The exact current 4,096-person ward phase histogram is reconstructed from the global population lineage plus sparse named exceptions without enumerating the ward. That histogram determines the ward's four-channel signature. The ward is then represented by a finite transducer

`C : B -> B x D`

where `D` is the additive public social output (`tales`, `warnings`, `contracts`, `feuds`).

No observer beyond the ward cut is allowed to inspect a hidden household directly. Under that declared interface, the transducer is the complete external behavior of the ward.

## 4. Exact spatial composition

If certificate `A` is crossed before certificate `B`, composition is

`(B o A)(x) = let (y, da) = A(x); let (z, db) = B(y); (z, da + db)`.

This operation is associative and generally noncommutative. Most importantly, it is closed: composing two 256-entry ward certificates produces another 256-entry certificate with the same boundary type.

Therefore:

- one ward has a 256-state boundary certificate;
- sixteen wards can collapse to one district certificate of the same width;
- sixteen district certificates can collapse to one 256-ward realm certificate of the same width;
- any larger ordered interior can continue collapsing without widening the external interface, provided the same cut contract remains valid.

The Court checks every one of the 256 possible external boundary inputs against literal ward-by-ward propagation across a 256-ward hierarchy.

## 5. Logarithmic local repair

Certificates are stored in a balanced separator tree. The root represents the entire interior. A changed ward replaces one leaf and recomputes only its ancestors.

For `W` leaves:

- one local certificate mutation requires `ceil(log2 W)` ancestor recompositions when W is a power of two;
- the root still exposes exactly 256 boundary states;
- applying the already-compiled realm certificate to one external state is one table lookup.

This is the spatial analogue of epochal history rebasing: local semantic change does not require replaying or resimulating the whole realm.

## 6. Lore is generated from authoritative structure

Household names, faction membership and structural neighbors are random-access deterministic functions of the realm seed and household ordinal. Gatewatch's Chronicle panel reads the actual population certificate and stable household graph.

The panel therefore does not invent an unrelated prose universe. A displayed trade partner or rival is a real recoverable graph relation, and household members are real citizen ordinals from the million-person address space.

Recent chronicle lines remain a bounded presentation/history cache. Long-term retrospective narrative beyond the retained cache would require a separate historical ledger; it must not be falsely reconstructed from current state.

## 7. What remains to prove

The next semantic-rank increases are harder because they break simple exchangeability:

- persistent marriages and births;
- arbitrary player-created friendship/grudge edges;
- personal inventories and wealth;
- household migration between wards;
- faction leadership and succession;
- rumor provenance rather than aggregate rumor level;
- schedules tied to physical businesses and homes;
- wars and trade flows over a branching geographic network rather than one ordered separator spine.

Each new semantic must either be included in a sufficient certificate, represented through a bounded separator, or promoted to a sparse explicit exception. The rule remains: **never compress away a fact that future gameplay is allowed to distinguish.**
